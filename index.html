Cheese, Shanghai edition

ALDI, Ole’ or Hema. Imported cheese, weird snacks and supermarket anthropology. A themed day can be extremely unserious.

Wander

Yuyuan Road loop

A shabbier-cooler alternative to simply marching down the famous streets. Cafés, lanes and old façades. Stop whenever something wins.

Feed me

Dumpling crawl

Pork shengjian, plain pork xiaolongbao, then pork chop with rice cakes if there is still room. Seafood versions stay off the order.

Little mission

Boba ladder

Not “find the best boba.” Try three genuinely different versions and develop an entirely unnecessary ranking system.

Anthropology

Convenience-store safari

One 7-Eleven or FamilyMart mission with a rule: each person chooses one thing the other would never have picked.

Wander

No destination walk

Pick a neighbourhood, take the first interesting side street, and let cafés and tiny shops decide the route.

Wednesday night

Ghost Market

Panjiayuan on 4 November is the research lead: old cameras, vintage toys, ephemera and haggling. Current night-market hours to be rechecked before we go.

Half-day thread

Lama Temple → hutongs

Temple, Wudaoying, Guozijian Street and fragrance-store browsing. The bracelet ritual is optional; the wandering is the point.

Seasonal

Go find the gold

Early-November ginkgo if the timing behaves. Choose the prettier current option once we can see the actual season.

Questionable snack

One brave bite

Fermented mung-bean drink, offal stew, tripe or something equally challenging. One attempt counts. Nobody has to finish anything.

Geometry

The 8D run

Monorail through a building, rooftop that is also ground level, impossible stairways. Collect examples of Chongqing refusing normal geometry.

Blue hour

Lower Hao instead

Research points to Xiahaoli for hillside lanes and bridge views. Hongya Cave can still have its postcard moment from across the river.

Time warp

Old tea house

Tea, sunflower seeds, card games, sketching students. Sit down. Observe. Do very little for a while.

Snack endgame

Grilled pig brain

Kimi has nominated this. Lynda Somewhere Else records the nomination without assuming anyone has actually agreed to it.

Dinner

Hotpot, but survivable

Go mild by Chongqing standards, order things we actually eat, and keep seafood, fish and raw onion out of the plan.

Research lead

Miffy investigation

The afternoon-tea hunt gets its own pocket until the Regent confirms what is actually running in November.

After dark

Market safari

Mong Kok, neon, goldfish, street markets and whatever catches the eye. This is a wander, not a shopping assignment.

Sunset

West Kowloon grass

Skyline from the lawn instead of joining a queue for a famous viewpoint. Add M+ if weather or energy says indoors.

Retail nonsense

DON DON DONKI hour

Snacks, cosmetics and inventions that raise more questions than they answer.

Food note

Hong Kong without seafood

Roast meats, beef noodles, pineapple bun, milk tea, egg waffles and milk pudding. Wonton noodles and fish balls are not our lane.

Andrew versus Margaret. Eat both. Judge with completely unjustified authority.

Camp Europe

Fake continent crawl

London, Paris and Venice without leaving Cotai. Lean into the ridiculousness rather than pretending otherwise.
